Airbnb’s new checkout rules · Nov 20

Your guests won’t do the dishes anymore. We will.

For reservations booked on or after November 20, 2026, Airbnb hosts can no longer require guests to do cleaning chores before they leave. That work now lands on every turnover.

DishesWashing and putting away
BedsStripping sheets
LaundryStarting a load
TrashTaking it off-site
Hosts can still ask, but not require, small things like leaving dishes in the sink or gathering towels.
